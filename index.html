Project Overview

QuestLogs is built to serve video game fans across all experience levels. Designed with a sleek dark-mode aesthetic, ambient RGB lighting accents, and high-contrast typography, the platform bridges hardware e-commerce with playable digital media.

Users can browse low-latency optical gaming gear, review news commentary, and play micro-games that critique modern corporate pricing structures.

Design Inspiration & Satirical Narrative

The narrative core of QuestLogs draws direct inspiration from Molleindustria's classic anti-corporate indie project, The McDonald's Video Game. While conventional e-commerce websites focus exclusively on transactional sales, QuestLogs uses interactive media as a form of social critique.

The fast-food satire games on QuestLogs mock how chains like Burger King, Starbucks, and Dunkin' have transitioned from affordable, everyday conveniences into luxury-priced expenses. Through business simulation mechanics, users manage fast-food chains—jacking up prices, cutting portion sizes, and managing public outrage—to examine economic elasticity firsthand.
