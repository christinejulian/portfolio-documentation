Project Overview

QuestLogs is an adaptive web application engineered to cater to gamers of all experience levels. Designed with a high-contrast dark-mode theme and glowing RGB accents, the platform delivers two primary user experiences: a professional esports hardware store and an interactive gaming portal featuring satirical simulations of modern fast-food price spikes.

Concept & Inspiration

The core philosophy behind QuestLogs takes direct inspiration from classic anti-corporate newsgames, such as The McDonald's Video Game. While conventional e-commerce platforms focus solely on retail transactions, QuestLogs presents e-commerce and gaming media as a form of social critique.

The original fast-food satire games on QuestLogs mock how fast-food chains like Burger King, Starbucks, and Dunkin' have transformed from affordable, everyday conveniences into overpriced luxury purchases. Users engage with tycoon-style mechanics where they experience corporate greed, inflation, and shrinkflation firsthand.
