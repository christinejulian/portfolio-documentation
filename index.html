Project Overview

QuestLogs is an adaptive web application engineered to cater to video game fans across all experience levels. Designed with a sleek dark-mode theme, glowing RGB lighting effects, and crisp typography, the platform mirrors the ambient aesthetics of modern gaming rigs while providing an intuitive, interactive environment.

Concept & Inspiration

The core concept behind QuestLogs draws inspiration from classical newsgames and anti-corporate satires, most notably The McDonald's Video Game. While conventional web projects focus purely on commercial transactions, QuestLogs presents digital media curation as a platform for social commentary.

Through interactive simulations and curated gameplay categories, QuestLogs directly critiques contemporary consumerism—specifically ridiculing how fast-food has shifted from a cheap, everyday convenience into an overpriced luxury over the past decade.
